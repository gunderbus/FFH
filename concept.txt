How the rpg system is going to work:
Weapons:
- Stats
- - Type || Different weapon types have different wielders
- - Weight || Weight determines the effect on speed
- - Damage || Damage is a added bonus to your stats, changes what bonus based on the different type of weapon

Armor:
- Stats
- - Type || Determines the type of wearer who can use it
- - Physical Defense || Boosts the defense of the character
- - Magical Defense

Characters:
- Stats
- - Weapon Type || Determines what type of weapon the weilder can use
- - Armor Type || Determines what type of armor the weilder can weare
- - Physical Defense
- - Max HP
- - Physical Attack
- - Magical Attack
- - Speed
